# Upstream provenance

VoxHearth is a privacy-focused fork of [TypeWhisper](https://github.com/TypeWhisper/typewhisper-mac).

The fork starts from TypeWhisper v1.5.1 at commit
`ea2843f2d179b0f43941e0fb2d3ed92d363eb31e`. VoxHearth replaces the upstream
name, artwork, product identity, distribution configuration, and application
surface. The source remains available under GPL-3.0-or-later.

TypeWhisper is a trademark of its respective owner. VoxHearth is independent
and is not endorsed by the TypeWhisper project.
